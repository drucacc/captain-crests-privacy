Privacy Policy

Captain Crests Inventory Sync

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. About this application

Captain Crests Inventory Sync is a private application used to synchronise product listing information from the Captain Crests eBay seller account with its Meta product catalogue.

2. Information accessed

The application accesses account-authorised eBay listing information, including:

  • Product titles and descriptions
  • Listing prices and currencies
  • Available quantities and listing status
  • Product images and listing URLs

The application uses eBay OAuth for authorised access and does not request or store your eBay password.

3. How information is used

Listing information is used solely to maintain and update the Captain Crests product catalogue on Meta platforms.

4. Data sharing

Listing information may be transmitted to Meta for catalogue management. Information is not sold or shared with unrelated third parties for advertising purposes.

5. Data storage and security

Application credentials and access tokens are stored locally on the application operator's computer. Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information from unauthorised access.

6. Data retention

Listing information is retained only as necessary to operate and maintain the product catalogue. Information that is no longer required may be deleted.

7. Third-party services

The application interacts with eBay and Meta services and is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.
